## manual.pdf (Dominate Grail War rulebook)
- Path: /mnt/project-files/uploads/hearth/c45602b1-c9df-4397-b466-affa648f311a (no extension; PDF). Uploaded by B 2026-10-25 with "ปรับปรุงกฎตามนี้ ออกแบบกระดานตามคู่มือ".
- 3,573,727 bytes, PDF 1.6, 20 A5 pages, Japanese, text layer extractable with `pdftotext -layout` (about 56 KB of text).
- It is the official rulebook of "Dominate Grail War -Fate/stay night on Board Game-" (DELiGHTWORKS, 2019, design BakaFire), 3-7 players. Not a Fate/Domination document.
- Pages: 2 opening, 3 overview + central board diagram, 4-5 components, 6-7 setup + round summary, 8-11 phase rules (prep, outpost, action incl. Command Spells p.10, combat, climax/elimination p.11), 12-13 casual rules, 14-17 Master and Servant blurbs (ability names only, no ability text), 18 glossary, 20 credits.
- Surprising: card images are low resolution, so card numbers, ability texts and move costs on arrows are not readable. Only sample cards on p.4 show power 2, 12 (an NP) and 5. Move totals are given in text (Workshop to Shinto 3, to Recon 5).
